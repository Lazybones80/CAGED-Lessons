01 · Read the lesson

What “harmonized” means here

Two layers are working together

The file pairs a chord arpeggio with a short scale passage. The arpeggio outlines the chord’s identity; the scale passage gives you a musical route into and out of those chord tones.

1 chord tones2 scale route3 voice-leading

How to use it with Guitar Pro

  1. Loop one 4–12 bar area at a slow tempo.
  2. Speak the root, third, fifth, and seventh before playing.
  3. Play the arpeggio alone, then connect it to the scale line.
  4. Raise the tempo only when the target chord tone lands cleanly.
!

C-form is a shape family, not a key. A D chord can use a C-shaped grip because the open C shape has been moved. The letter names tell you the harmony; the C-form tells you the fretboard geometry.

06 · Theory that stays honest

Triad, seventh, function, mode

Triad 1–3–5

A triad is the smallest complete chord identity. For example, D = D–F♯–A. Arpeggiate the three tones until the root and third feel automatic.

D major → D · F♯ · A

Seventh chord 1–3–5–7

The seventh adds color and function. Dmaj7 adds C♯; A7 uses a minor seventh, G, to create dominant pull back to D.

Two corrections to keep in your notes: F♯ Phrygian is the strict third mode of D major, but the GPX uses F♯ Dorian for a minor-chord sound. Also, C♯–E–G–A♯ is a fully diminished seventh; the diatonic seventh chord built on C♯ in D major is C♯–E–G–B (half-diminished).
